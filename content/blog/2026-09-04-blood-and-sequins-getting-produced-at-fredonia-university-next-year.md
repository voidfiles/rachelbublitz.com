So excited to share that [*Blood & Sequins*](https://www.brookpub.com/default.aspx?pg=sd&p=34980), my prom murder mystery, is getting a production with Fredonia University in March next year! If you're curious, you can check out their full 2026/27 season [right here](https://www.fredonia.edu/academics/colleges-schools/college-liberal-arts-sciences/theatre-dance/currpro).

*Blood & Sequins* will be directed by my friend [Daniel Lendzian](https://www.danlendzian.com/), who directed the world premiere of [*Burst*](https://www.dramaticpublishing.com/browse/burst) at [Alleyway Theatre](https://www.alleyway.com/) in Buffalo, New York a few years ago. Daniel directed [*The Book Women*](https://www.yourstagepartners.com/the-book-women) at Fredonia University as well, so this will mark our *third* collaboration. Repeat collaborators are so special to me, I'm so grateful to hear the my work resonates so strongly with Daniel and his students.

In or around Fredonia University? Then mark your calendars! *Blood & Sequins* will be performed March 4, 5, & 6 at 7:30 p.m., March 6 & 7 at 2:00 p.m. Want to find the script? Find it with [Brooklyn Publishers right here](https://www.brookpub.com/default.aspx?pg=sd&p=34980)!

Happy Friday!
