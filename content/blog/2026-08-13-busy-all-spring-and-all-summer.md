This last Spring, and soon to be ending Summer, has been *quite* busy for me. Busy with theater and plays yes, but also even more busy (mentally and emotionally) with my oldest child finishing high school! Tried not to cry through her final high school track and field meet. And her final high school water polo game. Or senior award night. Or graduation. That took care of my brain and bandwidth all Spring, basically.

And then comes Summer! I thought I would have more time and brain power, but Summer has been the countdown to her heading off to college. She's going across the country, we just started packing her boxes today.

So, long story short, I've been really quiet here on my blog! But, though my brain has been a good amount mush, the show must go on...

I have a play, soon to be announced, that's set to be published!

I broke pages on a brand new play, which I'm hoping to have finished the first draft of by the end of the month!

I attended the International Thespian Festival where [*The Night Witches*](https://www.dramaticpublishing.com/browse/the-night-witches) was a main stage production!

I flew out to Fort Lauderdale, Florida to see [New City Player's](https://newcityplayers.org/) production of [*Burst*](https://www.dramaticpublishing.com/browse/burst)!

And I have a number of exciting productions on the horizon that will be announced here very soon!

So, thanks for understanding. Big life changes!
