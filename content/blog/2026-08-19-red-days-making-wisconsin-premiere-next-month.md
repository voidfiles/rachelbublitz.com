Coming next month in September, [All In Productions](https://allin-mke.com/)' Wisconsin premiere of [*Red Days*](https://www.gitelmangoodpublishers.com/products/red-days?srsltid=AfmBOop2uV4jKePGFcwDqwUxTFFDbYm9hG0Ver1Qzseaf-NTogQ-XiZX)!

*Red Days* will run September 11 and run through September 19 at [Pink's Accessible Theatre House](https://www.google.com/maps/place/1104+W+Historic+Mitchell+St,+Milwaukee,+WI+53204/@43.0125686,-87.9283964,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x880519f056b3da71:0xb4676535bb650b5c!8m2!3d43.0125686!4d-87.9258215!16s%2Fg%2F11c2ccqbt8?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgxNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

The play will be directed by Erica Case, and will star Nessiah Grace, Brittany Haut, Graham Olen, and Patrice Hood. Interested in tickets? [Find 'em right here!](https://www.tixtree.com/e/red-days-282ba33d1fab)!

*Red Days* was just published earlier this year with [Gitelman and Good](https://www.gitelmangoodpublishers.com/?srsltid=AfmBOorRxPhhd65nKCIAHrAfTgrAkTd8-YZ9wRLZlwLZayB9CmljwykW).
