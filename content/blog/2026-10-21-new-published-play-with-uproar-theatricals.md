I am absolutely *thrilled* to announce that [*Presenting: Super Cat & Reptile Robot in the Tremendous Tickle Trouble*](https://www.uproartheatrics.com/shows/presenting-super-cat-and-reptile-robot) has been published with [Uproar Theatricals](https://www.uproartheatrics.com/)!!!

*Presenting: Super Cat & Reptile Robot in the Tremendous Tickle Trouble* was originally commissioned by [Plan-B Theatre](https://planbtheatre.org/) for their annual school tour. Because of Covid, Plan-B pivoted and produced the play has a really fun podcast. Since then Super Cat and Reptile Robot have gotten the opportunity to step on stage twice; once with Creative Arts Theatre Company in Tampa, Florida, and again with Birmingham Children's Theatre.

This play means a lot to me; it's my first play for a very young audience and it was inspired by my own kids' imagination game. Please read more about the play, and order yourself a perusal script, and maybe even license a production [right here](https://www.uproartheatrics.com/shows/presenting-super-cat-and-reptile-robot)!

Happy Wednesday!
