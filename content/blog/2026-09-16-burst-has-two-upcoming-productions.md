Thrilled to announce that coming up on the horizon [*Burst*](https://www.dramaticpublishing.com/browse/burst) has not one, but TWO upcoming productions! Sarah Boyd, the protagonist, is getting sh*t done and taking no prisoners.

Will either of these productions be near you? Check out the details:

*Burst* is the final play in [restless artists' theatre company](https://rattheatre.com/)'s 10th season in Sparks, Nevada! Opening October 30 through November 9.

The second production will be with [Spark Plug Theatre Collective](https://www.sparkplugtheatrecollective.org/)'s 4th season in Beaverton, Oregon! The play will run February 19-21 and 26-28, 2027.

Be sure to mark your calendar if you're near either theater, and if you've not close, maybe book some flights?

So happy to see productions of this play popping up around the country!
